Concept Maps

Hierarchical maps with feedback loop diagrams

Click any card to open it. With One branch at a time on, opening a card closes the others at that level, so you always see one level side by side instead of scrolling past everything you already opened. The trail above the map shows where you are, and clicking any step in it takes you back. Turn the button off if you would rather leave several branches open at once. Arrow keys work either way: right opens, left closes, up and down move between cards.

Start with explanations hidden and use the map to test yourself, then turn them on to check. Cards marked SEE point to the one place a concept is written out in full, including in another subject area.

      Reading the maps

      Each card carries a word in small capitals saying what kind of thing it is, and a second word saying how it relates to the card it came from.

      StructureA physical thing: organ, tissue, cell, or molecule.
      ProcessSomething happening: synthesis, transport, secretion, or action.
      ControlRegulation: a stimulus, a feedback step, a rhythm, or a set point.
      ClinicalWhere the card above it shows up in patient care.
      SeeA pointer to the one place the concept is written out in full.
